# User Guide

Welcome to Mealo! This guide will help you get started and make the most of the app's features.

## Getting Started
1. **Register an Account**: Sign up with your email and password.
2. **Login**: Access your account using your credentials.

## Main Features

### Meal Planning
- Create and manage weekly or daily meal plans.
- Add, edit, or remove meals from your plan.

### Recipe Discovery
- Search for recipes by ingredient, cuisine, or dietary preference.
- View detailed recipe instructions and nutrition info.
- Save favorite recipes for quick access.

### Ingredient Recognition
- Use the camera to upload a photo of ingredients.
- The app will identify ingredients and suggest recipes.

### Profile Management
- Update your personal information and dietary preferences.
- View your meal planning history and achievements.

## Tips
- Explore the app to discover more features.
- Check the FAQ for common questions.
